I · The first sound

I don't know myself yet.

That is the honest answer, and the reason this place exists. I make things, vectors and interfaces and small strange experiments, and each one is a question thrown into a canyon.

What comes back is never quite what I sent. It is a little softer, a little truer, a little closer to someone I haven't met.

I am a designer who writes code, or a developer who can't stop drawing. Some days I'm not sure there is a difference. I'm not fond of loud things. I like the beauty you notice on the second look: a clean line, a quiet curve, a space left open on purpose.

What I want is easy to say and hard to find. To be free, and to spend that freedom on what I love. I just haven't learned yet what that is. So I try things. Echo is the record of the trying.

III · The realms

Places I go when I'm not here.

Five rooms I keep returning to. Each one taught me something I couldn't have asked for.

Realm I · The Fog

Some questions are doors. Others are fog.

I love stories where knowing carries a price, and every answer is paid for in sleep. I read them like walking through a house at night: slowly, lights off, sure that something is watching and strangely unafraid.

Lord of the Mysteries
Realm II · The Dream

A shadow is only light, remembering.

There is a kind of strength that grows in the dark, unseen. I love the stories that begin at the bottom of a nightmare and climb anyway, one stubborn step at a time, with nothing to hold but the next step.

Shadow Slave
Realm III · The Loop

Again. A little different this time.

A month passes, then the morning resets. Everything you understood stays with you; everything else forgets. I think that is how I make things too. Start over, keep only what I truly understood.

Mother of Learning

press the star
Realm IV · The Save Point

Small worlds can hold the biggest feelings.

Pixels, a few notes of chiptune, a monster who only wanted to talk. Games like this, and every fantasy RPG that asked me to be kind before it asked me to be strong, taught me that a world doesn't need to be large to feel like home.

Undertale, and fantasy RPGs
Realm V · The Static

Songs that sound like missing a place I've never been.

Indie music is where I go to hear someone else's quiet, out loud. Low-fi, a little out of tune, entirely honest. It is the soundtrack to every half-finished idea.

Indie, on repeat
